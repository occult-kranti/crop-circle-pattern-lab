# DMT Patterns × Sacred Geometry × Crop Circles — Comparison Report

## Question
Do crop-circle geometries resemble DMT/psychedelic visual patterns and sacred-geometry motifs in a way that suggests a shared non-human origin — or a shared human one?

## Data & methods
- Crop-circle side: our curated 60-formation dataset + symmetry orders assigned per formation (`symmetry_orders.csv`) + verified reverse-cymatic decodes (cymatics lab).
- DMT side: Klüver form constants (1928/1966); Ermentrout & Cowan 1979 (Biol. Cybernetics) and Bressloff et al. 2001/2002 (Phil. Trans. R. Soc. B / Neural Computation) — log-polar V1 mapping turns cortical planforms (hexagonal/square/rolls) into tunnels, spirals, lattices, cobwebs; Lawrence et al. 2022 (Sci. Rep.) census of 3,778 DMT reports.
- Sacred-geometry side: motif provenance audit (vesica piscis & pentagram genuinely ancient; "Flower of Life"/"Metatron's Cube" names are 1990s New Age coinages; Sri Yantra 10th–11th c. Shri Vidya); Hawkins' crop-circle geometry work and its skeptical deflation (Markowsky 1992; N-gon identity (sec π/N)²).
- Psychology: apophenia/patternicity (Shermer 2008), loss-of-control → illusory patterns (Whitson & Galinsky 2008, Science), face pareidolia (Liu et al. 2014, Cortex).

## Findings
1. **Symmetry-space overlap is real and quantified.** 86.8% of assignable formations are n ≤ 6; dominated by radial, 3-, 4-, 6-fold — exactly the V1 planform families that generate form constants. DMT visual census: geometric patterns are the largest content class (32.2%), tunnels 10.3%, webs/grids 1.3%, mandalas 1.2%.
2. **Cymatic identity:** canonical Klüver patterns decoded with our engine coincide with crop-circle decodes — spiral = (1,1) = the 1990 dumbbell (406.56 Hz); honeycomb = (6,4) = the 2012 3-D cubes (2156.1 Hz). Same low-order mode space, as predicted.
3. **Sacred-geometry-named formations postdate the names.** "Flower of Life"/"Metatron's Cube" formations appear 2007+; the terms were coined in the 1990s (Melchizedek). Cultural transmission runs books → fields.
4. **Divergences are the signal:** legible information grids (23×73, ASCII), exact-iteration fractals (Koch/Mandelbrot — surveyed, staked), high-order symmetry (n=10/12/18/44) — none occur in the Klüver repertoire or traditional sacred geometry. These require design tools and intent.
5. **The entity parallel:** 45.5% of DMT reports include entities (96% rated intelligent/benevolent — Davis 2020); the fields got faces/aliens in 2001–02. Humans project agents in both media.

## Conclusion
All three systems draw on the same neuro-geometric wellspring (rings, spirals, hexagons, quincunxes), which is why crop circles *feel* like visions and temples. But the formations systematically exceed that wellspring (bits, exact fractals, high primes, culture-stamped motifs) — precisely the signature of skilled human makers fluent in both mathematics and the sacred-geometry canon. Shared geometry is evidence of shared human minds, not shared cosmic sources.
