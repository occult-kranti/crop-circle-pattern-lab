The crop-circle phenomenon, measured.

A curated dataset of 60 major formations, 19 research papers and a century of aggregate counts — analyzed with open-source tools (Python, pandas, SciPy, scikit-learn, SQLite) and presented as a fully local, dependency-free website. Every number is flagged as hard data, researcher estimate, or media claim.

Seven eras

How the phenomenon evolved — from folklore pamphlet to corporate commissions.

Precursors

1678 Mowing-Devil pamphlet · 1880 Nature letter · 1966 Tully 'saucer nest' (Australia) — the event that inspired Doug Bower.

Early circles 1978–89

Bower & Chorley's plank-and-rope circles in Hampshire; press coverage from 1980; epicentre shifts to Wiltshire/Avebury by 1988.

Pictogram era 1990–95

Complex non-circular designs; East Field 'Led Zeppelin' pictogram; Operation Blackbird humiliation; Doug & Dave confess Sept 1991.

Fractal golden age 1996–2000

Stonehenge Julia Set (151 circles), Triple Julia, Koch snowflakes — mathematical sophistication peaks.

Peak & message era 2001–02

Milk Hill Galaxy (409 circles), Chilbolton Face + 'Arecibo reply', Crabwood ASCII alien — and the movie Signs.

Math & mega era 2004–10

Barbury Castle pi wheel, Euler's identity, 3-D illusions, 600-ft jellyfish; decline begins ~2009.

Commercial/decline 2011–25

Nvidia's Chualar stunt, Supreme, Korn; UK counts fall to dozens/yr; farmers cut circles from 2012; tourism economy persists.
