Archaeoacoustics · forward modeling

Temples, pyramids and monuments as resonators.

Every monument below is modeled with room-mode physics (f = c/2·√Σ(nᵢ/Lᵢ)²), Helmholtz and diffraction-comb estimators, then annotated with the published in-situ measurements where they exist. Every frequency carries a provenance tag: MEASURED in-situ literature value · MODELED computed from dimensions · CONTESTED measured but challenged.

The "110 Hz canon" — adjudicated

Modeled chamber fundamentals (first axial modes)

All small stone chambers cluster at 15–60 Hz fundamentals with upper modes spanning 95–120 Hz — dimensionally inevitable for 1.5–12 m rooms, so bare frequency matching is not evidence of design intent. What distinguishes sites is mode order, degeneracy structure, and decay times.

Ground truth: Jahn, Devereux & Ibison 1996 (JASA 99:649) · Till 2017 (Antiquity 91:74) · Lubman 1998 (JASA 104:1763) · Cox, Fazenda & Greaney 2020 (J. Arch. Sci. 122:105218) · Cook et al. 2008/2010 (Chavín). Modeling spec: panel physics consultation (rigid-box modes, Helmholtz, combs). Full data: website assets load monuments.json.
