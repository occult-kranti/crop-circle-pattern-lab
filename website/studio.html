7 · Honesty note

Model estimates, not hidden music. Every formation frequency shown here is a model estimate — geometry mapped onto candidate drum modes and an assumed 30 cm reference membrane — with confidence LOW or MEDIUM on real photographs (only the printed 23×73 Chilbolton grid rates HIGH). Monument values are measured in situ or modeled from dimensions, each with a cited source. None of this is evidence that formations encode sound; it is a demonstration of how geometry, vibration and pitch interrelate — and of how easily patterns invite over-reading.
Reference resonator R1: 30 cm drumhead, c = 100 m/s (f₀₁ = 255.2 Hz). 12-TET, A4 = 440 Hz. Engine adapted from Open Sync (brainwave_opensync), MIT License. Self-test: assets/studio_selftest.html.
