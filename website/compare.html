Comparative analysis

Same geometry, three origins?

Crop-circle formations, DMT/psychedelic form constants, and sacred-geometry motifs all draw on spirals, concentric rings, hexagonal lattices and radial webs. This page quantifies the overlap using our 60-formation dataset, the largest DMT content census (Lawrence et al. 2022, n=3,778), the V1 cortex model of hallucination geometry (Ermentrout–Cowan 1979; Bressloff et al. 2001/2002), and the sacred-geometry literature — and marks precisely where the three systems diverge.

Frame. Shared motifs are weak evidence of shared cause: the Bressloff–Cowan model predicts exactly these symmetry families (radial, spiral, hexagonal/square lattice) from the wiring of the human visual cortex, and circle-makers are humans with visual cortices. The interesting signal is in the divergences.

Symmetry order (n-fold) across the 60-formation dataset

86.8% of assignable formations have n ≤ 6. The distribution is dominated by radial (n=0: rings/nests), 3-, 4- and 6-fold designs — exactly the wallpaper/axial families the V1 model predicts for hallucination geometry (hexagonal and square planforms are the dominant cortical bifurcations).

Visual content of 3,778 DMT experiences (Lawrence et al. 2022)

Geometric content is the largest single category. Tunnels, webs/grids, spirals and mandalas — the Klüver classes — recur; explicit "sacred geometry" vocabulary is reported spontaneously (0.6%).

Where the systems diverge — and what that proves

1 · Information content

No hallucination or mandala tradition produces a legible 1,679-bit binary grid (Chilbolton 2001) or ASCII text (Crabwood 2002). Information-bearing glyphs are outside the Klüver repertoire entirely — designed, not neurogenic.

2 · Exact mathematics

DMT "fractals" (32.6% of reports) are loosely self-similar textures; crop fractals are exact iterations (Koch snowflakes, Mandelbrot, Julia) requiring stake-point surveying — a surveying firm estimated 4–5 days + 340 stakes to reproduce the 1997 Koch.

3 · High-order symmetry

Form constants are low-order (V1 lattice: 4/6-fold). Formations reach n=10 (pi wheel), n=12 (Euler wheel), n=18 (Potterne 2023), n=44 (pi mandala) — far beyond cortical planforms, implying deliberate design tools.

4 · Cultural timestamping

"Flower of Life" and "Metatron's Cube" are 1990s New Age coinages (Melchizedek 1998/99); formations bearing those names appear 2007+. The vocabulary entered the fields after the books — cultural transmission into human art, not ancient wisdom out of the fields.

5 · The entities parallel

45.5% of DMT reports include entities; crop circles got faces (2001–02) and alien iconography right after Signs. Both tracks show humans projecting agents — one neurochemically, one in wheat.

6 · Hawkins' theorems, deflated

Hawkins' celebrated diatonic ratios and "new Euclidean theorems" reduce to the elementary N-gon identity (sec π/N)² — and Hawkins himself concluded the makers were human ("unusual cleverness"). Geometry literacy ≠ alien origin.

Synthesis

The three pattern systems overlap exactly where human neuroarchitecture and compass geometry overlap: rings, spirals, hexagons, quincunxes — the same families our cymatic decoder collapses onto identical modes. They diverge exactly where design exceeds neurology: bits, codes, exact fractals, high-order symmetry, and culture-stamped motifs. The pattern evidence therefore points the same way as the confessions, the GIS statistics and the timeline: crop circles are a human art form drawing on the same geometric wellspring as sacred art and psychedelic vision — which is precisely why they feel numinous.
