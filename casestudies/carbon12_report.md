# Case Study 01 — The "Carbon-12 Decode" Video: Scientific Adjudication

## The claim
A ~45-minute video argues that a "2000 series of 50 crop circles" is not art but an extraterrestrial physics lesson about the atom — specifically Carbon-12's "three-alpha lock" (three helium-4 nuclei in the nucleus) — that formations reveal "a missing geometric detail" linking gravity into atomic structure, and that the narrator reads them via an alien brain-computer implant (Cancún, Feb 2012), with a recurring "2.17 mm X-ray measurement."

## Method
Three parallel research streams (source forensics; nuclear physics; 2000-season census) + cross-check against our 60-formation dataset and symmetry analysis.

## Verdicts

| # | Sub-claim | Classification |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Three helium-4 nuclei form carbon-12 | ✅ Consistent (triple-alpha process, Salpeter 1952) |
| 2 | A special resonance enables carbon formation | ✅ Consistent (Hoyle state, 0⁺, 7.654 MeV; Dunbar et al. 1953; Hoyle 1954) |
| 3 | "Three-alpha lock" — He-4 nuclei "bond" in C-12 | ⚠️ Garbled real science — no such term; no "bonds" in nuclei; the Hoyle state is unbound (~10⁻¹⁶ s) |
| 4 | C-12 has triangular 3α geometry | ⚠️ Real but anachronistic — compact triangle (ground state) / bent-arm (Hoyle state) published 2012–2023 (Epelbaum PRL 2012; Shen Nat. Commun. 2023), after the circles |
| 5 | Circles reveal a missing geometric detail | ❌ Unsupported — real depiction is an energy-level diagram (MeV, J^π, thresholds); no glyph content anticipated later results |
| 6 | Gravity links into atomic structure | ❌ Unsupported — gravity/EM = 8×10⁻³⁷ at 1 fm; ~37 orders of magnitude off |
| 7 | "2.17 mm X-ray measurement" | ❌ Fabricated/untraceable — absent from Levengood 1994/1999 (micrometer ratios, not X-ray); nearest real figure: 2.0 mm control node length (Haselhoff 2014); X-ray work = clay XRD |
| 8 | "2000 series of 50 crop circles" | ❌ Disproven by data — archives: 125 (Connector) / 139 (ICCA) in England, 175–253 worldwide; no 50-series; season themes were triangles, moiré/grids, 11-fold, π — zero atomic interpretations at the time (element-coded formation is Chilbolton, Aug **2001**) |
| 9 | Alien implant (Cancún 2012) | ⬜ Unfalsifiable — no checkable evidence; makes the "decode" circular by design |

## Key facts established
- **2000 season counts:** 125 England + 67 international (Crop Circle Connector archive); 139 England / 253 worldwide (ICCA Annual Report 2000, Andreas Müller); 175 worldwide (Colin Andrews). Lucy Pringle photographed 57.
- **2000 headliners:** Avebury Trusloe "Moiré" (read by Alan Holt as a bar magnet — the season's only physics-flavoured moment); Chilbolton fractal (13 Aug); Woodborough Hill 22-fold "Sunflower"/π mandala (13 Aug); West Overton seven-pointed star (24 Aug — Matthew Williams' formation; first UK conviction, £100 + £40, Nov 2000); BBC (9 Aug 2000): Andrews' 80%-man-made estimate.
- **Real C-12 physics:** ground state = compact 3α triangle; Hoyle state (0⁺, 7.654 MeV) = bent-arm/obtuse configuration, decays to 3α in ~10⁻¹⁶ s, radiative branch 0.04%; fine-tuning quantified by Oberhummer et al. (Science 289:88, 2000).
- **Source forensics:** the video has zero indexed footprint; "three-alpha lock" and "2.17 mm" return no hits; no Reddit/Metabunk/ATS discussion exists. Closest genre relatives: IASOR "TIFACS" proposal, Pitkänen's TGD, Horace Drew decodes.
- **Dataset cross-check:** no 3-fold/atomic burst in 2000; our 3-fold designs cluster 1991–1997.

## Conclusion
The video dresses a genuine marvel (the Hoyle state) in fabricated terminology, a meaningless gravity link, a non-existent measurement, and an archive-contradicted count. Verdict: **DISPROVEN** on all checkable claims, with an **unfalsifiable** narrative core. The real physics needs no embellishment.
